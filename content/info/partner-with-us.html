Partner With Us

Since 1975, Kai Ming has worked alongside families, schools, community organizations, and local businesses to give San Francisco’s children a strong start. Our partners help us reach more families and bring new experiences into our classrooms.

Ways to partner

  • Community organizations: co-host family events and workshops, and connect families with the services they need.
  • Schools and colleges: offer student placements and early educator training, and help children move smoothly into kindergarten.
  • Businesses and foundations: sponsor classroom resources, enrichment programs, or special projects.
  • In-kind support: donate books, learning materials, and supplies for children and families.

Let’s work together

To talk about a partnership, contact us and tell us a little about your organization. Our team will follow up with next steps.
